Stream Day
Thursday is the heartbeat. Gigi goes live at 4:00 PM PT and the whole week's pipeline arrives in about two hours. This is the run of show, plus a shared to-do list and notes for the team.
Run of Show (Thursday)
3:30 PM
Confirm coverage for the application wave
Who is on triage tonight, who takes qualified applications first, who covers the New Calls channel. Every hot lead needs an owner before the room opens.
3:55 PM
Someone from the team is in the Zoom room
Watch the chat, catch tech issues, and be ready to drop the apply link.
~4:40 PM
The pitch lands. Apply link in chat within 4 minutes.
Every minute of delay costs applications. The week it took 8 minutes, applications per attendee dropped 15 points. Under 4 is the target.
5:00 PM on
Speed to lead on every qualified application: under 15 minutes
Qualified applications ping #qualified-app. Call, then text. A setter call cuts no-shows from 22% to 8%.
5:00 PM on
Triage every new booking tonight
Bookings ping New Calls. Every booking is auto-marked confirmed by the system, so that field means nothing. A real YES from the lead is the only confirmation. Triage is a setter job, never the closer who owns the call.
Midnight
Every hot lead has an owner and a next touch
Unbooked qualified applicants and anyone who cancelled go into tomorrow's chase. In September these got zero outreach. That is the biggest leak after checkout.
Every call
End of Call form within 10 minutes
Feeds pay, the dashboard and coaching. The form. Show or no-show, one per call.
